# Accounts, cards and deposits

Accounts and cards are added from your bank messages. Tap **+** on the Accounts screen to add one by hand (bank, last 4 digits, type, network, balance or limit).

## Edit an account or card
- Nickname, colour, personal or business use.
- Type: savings, current, FD, RD, PPF, loan, credit, debit or prepaid card.
- Debit cards link to the bank account they draw from.
- Cards: **forex markup %** for spends in other currencies.

## FD and RD
Set the maturity date and what happens then:
- **Pay out to account**: the deposit leaves your list on that date.
- **Renew** (principal, or principal and interest): it stays.

Tap any account to see its spending and income over 6 months, a year or all time.
