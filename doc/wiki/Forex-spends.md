# Forex spends

Spends in USD and other currencies count in your totals once they have a rupee value.

1. Settings › **Forex rates** › **+**: choose the currency, the date and the rupees for one unit.
2. Each spend uses the rate of its own date, or the nearest date you set.
3. The card's forex markup (Accounts › edit card) is added on top. Include GST: 3.5% + 18% = 4.13%.

The Forex rates page shows how many spends in each currency still need a rate.
