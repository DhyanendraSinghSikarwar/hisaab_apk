# Getting started

1. Install the APK from Releases and open Hisaab.
2. Enter your full name and sign in with email (connects your inbox for bank alerts) or a mobile number.
3. Add your date of birth and PAN. Banks lock statements with these; Hisaab uses them only to open your PDFs.
4. Allow SMS access. Hisaab reads past bank messages once, then new ones as they arrive.

## Tabs
| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Home | The month's budgets, categories, subscriptions and activity. The month switcher applies to every section. |
| History (₹) | Every transaction, with search and filters. Bills calendar at the top right. |
| Portfolio | Investments, deposits and statements. |
| Analytics | Spent, income, saved and per-day, then categories, merchants and trends. |

Settings › Customize tabs lets you hide or reorder sections.
